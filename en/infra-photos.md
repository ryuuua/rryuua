  <section class="card">
    <h2>Photo Plan</h2>
    <p>
      This page is reserved for the visual side of the cluster: machines, cabling,
      storage, cooling, and the small details that never show up in a spec table.
      The goal is not only to document hardware, but to show the personality of the setup.
    </p>
    <p>
      When real images are added, keep them lightweight and responsive like the home
      gallery: export optimized variants first, then add short captions that explain
      why the detail matters.
    </p>
  </section>
